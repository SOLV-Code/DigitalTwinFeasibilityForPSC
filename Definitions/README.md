# Definitions


## Digital Twin Jargon

* **Digital Twin**: We broadly define a digital twin as a simulation model with continuous updates from real-time data, at time intervals appropriate to the model’s scope and purpose. This is the definition used in an ecological context ([de Koning et al. 2023](https://github.com/SOLV-Code/DigitalTwinFeasibilityForPSC/tree/main/AnnotatedBibliography#de-koning-et-al-2023)). Digital twins serve the dual purpose of (1) communicating a system’s present state and (2) supporting exploration of predicted states, based on underlying models of case-specific structure and complexity. Increasing use of digital twins across domains such as engineering and emergency response indicates that this new approach has the potential to improve how salmon models are developed, deployed, and used for decisions.



## PSC Jargon

* **Northern Diversion**: A variable proportion of returning adult Fraser River sockeye migrate along a northern route around Vancouver Island through Johnstone Strait, while the remainder migrates along a southern route along the Westcoast of Vancouver Island through Juan de Fuca Strait. The northern diversion rate affects catchability in different fisheries and the portion of the return migrating through U.S. fisheries, but also affects the interpretation of in-season assessment surveys, such as test fisheries. Both run timing and diversion rate are linked to marine conditions and clear differences between individual Fraser sockeye stocks have been observed.
