# Fraser Sockeye Timing and Northern Diversion


## Context

A variable proportion of returning adult Fraser River sockeye migrate along a northern route around Vancouver Island through Johnstone Strait, while the remainder migrates along a southern route along the Westcoast of Vancouver Island through Juan de Fuca Strait. The northern diversion rate affects catchability in different fisheries and the portion of the return migrating through U.S. fisheries, but also affects the interpretation of in-season assessment surveys, such as test fisheries. Both run timing and diversion rate are linked to marine conditions and clear differences between individual Fraser sockeye stocks have been observed.
